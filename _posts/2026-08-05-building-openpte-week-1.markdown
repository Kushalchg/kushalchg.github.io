![Openpte Read aloud scoring and feedback ui](/assets/images/week1/openpte-rl-scoring.png)

This week on [Openpte](https://openpte.com) I managed to build and deploy a 
`Read Aloud` which is our first question of the speaking type on Openpte. Now I am working
on the other speaking question types like `Repeat Sentence` , `Describe Image`
and other, these will be available in coming days maybe before week-2.

The scoring algorithm for speaking is already on par with the current market
platforms like Alfapte but I think it still need more improvement on content and
pronunciation area, you can [check and practice](https://app.openpte.com/) and
may be provide some feedback about what you feel off. But if you want to know
more about how scoring algorithm works for `Read Aloud` and other speaking questions, [you
can reach out.](mailto:kushalchapagian123456@gmail.com) But in brief it scores based on the [guidline](https://www.pearsonpte.com/content/dam/ELL/pte/pearsonpte/pdfs/pte-academic-pdfs/PTE-Academic-Test-Taker-Score-Guide.pdf) that [Pearson](https://www.pearsonpte.com/) provide for scoring.

Currently the number of questions availabe for speaking are only about 50 but
will be added regularly once the scoring and feedback system became robust.
By default the normal user will get/have `20` AI submission for `Writing` and `Speaking`
but you are practicing and require more, never be shy to [reach out to me](mailto:kushalchapagian123456@gmail.com). I am constantly looking for feedback and suggestion.
